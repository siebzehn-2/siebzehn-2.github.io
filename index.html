A short version of how I got here and what I actually spend my days doing.

I started out fixing phones and messing with cameras, which turned into freelance photography, then into editing other people's footage, then into running whole social accounts. Somewhere in there "media guy" became the job title.

These days most of my work is same-day event edits, short-form reels for Facebook and TikTok, and the social calendar behind them. I'm also teaching myself workflow automation, so the boring parts of running a content pipeline — bookings, approvals, handoffs — take less of everyone's time.
